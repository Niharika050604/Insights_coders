# Electric Vehicle Sales Analysis ⚡🚗

A Power BI data analysis project exploring electric vehicle (EV) sales trends across Indian states and manufacturers.

| `electric_vehicle_sales_by_state.xlsx` | EV sales data by Indian state |
| `dim_date.xlsx` | Date dimension table for time-based analysis |
| `practical - 4.pbix` | Power BI dashboard with visualizations |
| `Practical_4_Word_Doc.docx` | Written analysis and findings |

## 📊 Objective
To analyze the growth and distribution of electric vehicle sales across India — broken down by state and manufacturer — to identify market trends and leading players in the EV sector.

## 🔍 Key Areas Explored
- Top EV manufacturers by total sales volume
- State-wise EV adoption trends across India
- Year-over-year growth in EV sales
- Comparison of 2-wheeler vs 4-wheeler EV market
- Identifying leading and lagging states in EV penetration

## 💡 Why This Project Matters
India's EV market is one of the fastest-growing in the world. This analysis provides valuable insights for businesses, policymakers, and investors looking to understand the evolving landscape of sustainable transportation in India.
